1. Flash the ISO to USB & boot

Download the official CachyOS image from https://cachyos.org/ (reference: official docs). Back up anything on your USB stick first because flashing overwrites it.

balenaEtcher (Windows, macOS, Linux)

  1. Open balenaEtcher, click Flash from file, and pick the downloaded CachyOS ISO.
  2. Click Select target and choose your USB flash drive (verify the size/name so you do not pick an external backup drive).
  3. Click Flash! and wait for flashing and verification to complete.

Rufus (Windows)

  1. Plug in the USB stick, open Rufus, and select your USB drive under Device.
  2. Click SELECT next to Boot selection and choose your CachyOS ISO file.
  3. Click START, confirm the data-erase warning, and wait until Rufus shows Ready.

Booting the USB: Restart and press your boot menu key (often F12, F11, F8, or Esc), select the USB drive, and disable Fast Boot / Secure Boot / Windows Fast Startup if needed.

2. CachyOS installer & pacman setup

Boot the CachyOS ISO and launch the installer.

  1. Select desktop environment, bootloader, and target partition layout (Btrfs or ext4).
  2. Configure timezone, locale, hostname, and user account credentials.
  3. Complete installation and reboot into the new system.

Commands for CachyOS

sudo pacman -Syu uname -r cat /proc/cpuinfo | grep -m1 'model name'

3. After installation

Arch & pacman rolling-release notes

Arch and Arch-based distros update continuously. Keep a fallback kernel such as linux-lts installed and avoid partial upgrades.

sudo pacman -Syu

Practical tips for CachyOS

1. Test default installer selections first before changing kernel or scheduler flavors.

2. Keep a working fallback kernel available in the bootloader.

4. Troubleshooting

USB not booting: Re-flash the ISO with balenaEtcher or Rufus, try another USB port, and check UEFI boot priority and Secure Boot / Fast Boot settings.

Installer or boot issue: Check the official CachyOS documentation for exact partition, driver, or bootloader steps, and save your own notes in the sidebar.
