1. Flash the ISO to USB & boot

Download the official Manjaro Linux image from https://manjaro.org/download/ (reference: official docs). Back up anything on your USB stick first because flashing overwrites it.

balenaEtcher (Windows, macOS, Linux)

  1. Open balenaEtcher, click Flash from file, and pick the downloaded Manjaro Linux ISO.
  2. Click Select target and choose your USB flash drive (verify the size/name so you do not pick an external backup drive).
  3. Click Flash! and wait for flashing and verification to complete.

Rufus (Windows)

  1. Plug in the USB stick, open Rufus, and select your USB drive under Device.
  2. Click SELECT next to Boot selection and choose your Manjaro Linux ISO file.
  3. Click START, confirm the data-erase warning, and wait until Rufus shows Ready.

Booting the USB: Restart and press your boot menu key (often F12, F11, F8, or Esc), select the USB drive, and disable Fast Boot / Secure Boot / Windows Fast Startup if needed.

2. Manjaro Calamares setup & pacman maintenance

Boot the Manjaro live ISO and click Launch Installer on the welcome screen.

  1. Pick location/timezone and keyboard layout in Calamares.
  2. Choose Erase disk or Manual partitioning (with EFI mount /boot/efi if on UEFI) and create your user account.
  3. Click Install, wait for package extraction and bootloader configuration to finish, and reboot.

Commands for Manjaro Linux

sudo pacman -Syu mhwd-kernel -li sudo pacman -S --needed base-devel git

3. After installation

Arch & pacman rolling-release notes

Arch and Arch-based distros update continuously. Keep a fallback kernel such as linux-lts installed and avoid partial upgrades.

sudo pacman -Syu

Practical tips for Manjaro Linux

1. Use mhwd-kernel or Manjaro Settings Manager when switching kernels.

2. Keep important data backed up before large rolling updates.

4. Troubleshooting

USB not booting: Re-flash the ISO with balenaEtcher or Rufus, try another USB port, and check UEFI boot priority and Secure Boot / Fast Boot settings.

Installer or boot issue: Check the official Manjaro Linux documentation for exact partition, driver, or bootloader steps, and save your own notes in the sidebar.
